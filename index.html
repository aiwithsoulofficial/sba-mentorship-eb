The order we would do this in

We are not going to go rushing at the psychic and healing side of things. You told me this morning you are not looking to do this professionally, and I heard you. Nothing in here will nudge you in that direction, and if it ever changes it will be because you decided it, not because I steered you there.

We go in this order, and we do not skip ahead:

First we settle you.The body, the nervous system, the sleep, the overstimulation. While your system is running on alarm nothing else I hand you will land properly, so we start here even though it is the least interesting part of the whole thing.
Then we release.The grief, the old traumas you said you are starting to think you might need to look at, the anger that was there long before any of this happened. Slowly, and never faster than you can hold.
Then we reconnect you to you.Who you are underneath the autopilot, what you like, what your body is telling you. The yes and no comes back online somewhere in here, because by then there is enough quiet for you to hear it.
Only then do we expand.The dreams, the signs, whatever is already trying to reach you, and only as far as you want to take it. That might mean going a long way with it, or it might mean going just far enough that it stops unsettling you when it happens. Either one is a good result.

If we get to the three month mark and you have only worked through the first two, we have still done the work. That is your system taking the pace it could take.

A word about your nervous system

You mentioned the ADHD and the autism traits almost in passing this morning, and then went straight back to apologising for how you react to things. I want to come back to that for a minute.

Nobody has ever sat you down and asked whether decision making should even look the same for you as it does for everybody else. You have been told your whole life how to operate. Write a pros and cons list. Trust your gut. Just decide, stop overthinking it. All of that was built by people whose systems work differently to yours, and then handed to you as though it were universal.

Most of us get pushed into the same mould, and for a lot of us it does real harm. The part that gets missed is what comes afterwards, because years later we are still shoving ourselves back into that shape with nobody standing over us making us do it, because somewhere along the way we were taught that is what a functioning adult looks like. It is where the people pleasing lives, and the yes to shifts you do not want, and the checking in with three people before you will let yourself move.

So we work the other way around. I look at your human design and your astrology because they show me how you are wired to make decisions, and then we build around that instead of against it. If your system needs a few days and a decent sleep before it can commit to anything, that is your design, and we stop treating it as something to be corrected.

I am neurospicy myself, so I will also tell you that learning to read your own nervous system takes practice. It is a skill and skills need repetition. You will get it wrong, you will override it, and you will notice on a Thursday that your body already told you on Monday. All of that is the process, and it is why we do this over months rather than in a weekend.

Which one

Either of these will do the work. What changes is how much of me you have while you are doing it.

If you want my honest read, and you can take it or leave it, the weekly one is what I would put you in. You told me the heavy days come often, that you cannot make decisions without running every what if, and that you have nobody who does this for you. Twelve weeks of weekly contact means the tools get embedded instead of forgotten, and it means that on the day something knocks you sideways you can reach me that day rather than sitting in it for three weeks waiting for our next session.

The monthly one is a genuinely good container, and it suits you if you like your own space, want time to sit with things between sessions, and would rather not add another standing commitment to your week while you are in the middle of all this.
