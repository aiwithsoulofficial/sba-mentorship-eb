The order we would do this in

This matters, so I want to say it plainly. We are not going to charge at your gifts. You told me you are not looking to do this professionally, and I heard you. Nothing in here is going to push you towards that.

First, we settle you.The body, the nervous system, the sleep, the overstimulation. Nothing else lands while you are running on alarm. This is the quiet, unglamorous part and it is the part that changes everything.
Then, we release.Gently. The grief, the old traumas you said you might need to look at, the anger that was there long before December. At your pace, never faster than you can hold.
